More about me

Hobbies

Badminton. I love playing badminton. I won the team gold medal at the Shaoxing Badminton Championships and reached the top four in both singles and doubles. My favorite players are Shi Yuqi and Kento Momota. If you'd like to play a game together, feel free to reach out on WeChat!

Photography. I like observing the world through photography, especially street photography and nature landscapes.

Calligraphy. I practice Chinese calligraphy. I have studied Zhiyong's Thousand Character Classic (智永《千字文》), Wang Xizhi's Preface to the Orchid Pavilion Collection (《兰亭序》) and Yan Zhenqing's Draft of a Requiem to My Nephew (《祭侄文稿》). My favorite calligrapher is Su Shi (Su Dongpo, 苏轼), especially his Cold Food Observance in Huangzhou (《黄州寒食诗帖》). I love his grand, free-spirited style with bold, expressive ink.

To me, badminton and calligraphy have something in common: both are full of rhythm (韵律).
