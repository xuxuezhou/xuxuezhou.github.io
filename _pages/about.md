I'm currently a research assistant and incoming PhD student at [MARS Lab](https://marslab.tech/contact/), working with [Prof. Jianfei Yang](https://marsyang.site/) and [Dr. Wenyu Liang](https://www.liangwenyu.com/).

I completed my B.Sc. in Mathematics (Highest Distinction) with a minor in Computer Science at NUS. I was fortunate to work with [Prof. Benjamin Riviere](https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/benjamin-riviere), [Prof. Xingyu Liu](https://xingyul.github.io/), [Prof. Jun Zhu](https://ml.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/~jun/index.shtml) and [Prof. Hang Su](http://www.suhangss.me/).

## Research Interests

My current research focuses on world models in robotics: helping robots learn how the world works, so they can predict, plan and act more intelligently.

My long-term interest is to study the brain 🧠. I want to understand the fundamental logic of cognition from the perspectives of cognitive science, psychology and biology, and use those insights to build the next generation of robot brains.
